Architecture & System Questions

Key architectural decisions, keyboard access rules, and vanilla frontend principles behind NEXORA.

NEXORA is engineered strictly on pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern Vanilla JavaScript without external runtimes, heavy frameworks, or bloated bundle sizes. This yields near-zero first-input delay, instant client-side rendering, and complete control over accessibility semantics.

Every interactive element adheres to W3C WAI-ARIA standards. Tabs support Arrow Left/Right, Home, and End navigation; modals trap focus and restore focus on Escape or close; accordion triggers reflect real-time aria-expanded states; and color is never used as the sole conveyor of information.
